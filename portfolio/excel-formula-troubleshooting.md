# Excel Formula Fix & Error Troubleshooting

## What I solve
I troubleshoot existing Excel workbooks, find the root cause of incorrect results, fix formulas and references, and validate the final output without unnecessarily rebuilding the file.

### Typical issues
- `#REF!`, `#VALUE!`, `#N/A` and other Excel errors
- XLOOKUP / VLOOKUP problems
- SUMIFS / COUNTIFS / IF / IFERROR logic
- Broken or shifted references
- Inconsistent formulas across rows or periods
- Reconciliation differences
- Incorrect calculations based on business rules
- Small validation and automation improvements

## My process
1. Review the workbook and reproduce the issue.
2. Confirm the expected business result.
3. Trace formulas, references and dependencies.
4. Identify the root cause.
5. Fix the formula or spreadsheet logic.
6. Validate the corrected result.
7. Deliver the updated workbook with a short explanation of what changed.

## Deliverables
- Corrected Excel workbook
- Fixed formulas and references
- Validation/reconciliation checks where relevant
- Short root-cause explanation

## Tools
Microsoft Excel · XLOOKUP · SUMIFS · IF / IFERROR · Data Validation · Reconciliation
